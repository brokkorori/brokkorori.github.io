Image Rotation Tool (90°, 180°, 270°)

This image rotation tool allows you to rotate images 90°, 180°, or 270° directly in your browser. All processing is done locally — your images never leave your device. Supports batch rotation and drag & drop for easy use.

When to Use Image Rotation

  • Photos taken on smartphones appear sideways
  • Scanned documents need orientation correction
  • Images must be aligned for blogs or presentations
